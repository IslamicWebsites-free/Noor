The Five Pillars of Islam

The foundation of a Muslim's faith and practice.

1

Shahada

Declaring that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad ﷺ is His Messenger.

2

Salah

Performing the five daily prayers, connecting the believer with Allah.

3

Zakat

Giving a set portion of wealth to those in need, purifying one's earnings.

4

Sawm

Fasting from dawn to sunset during the month of Ramadan.

5

Hajj

Pilgrimage to Makkah once in a lifetime for those who are able.
